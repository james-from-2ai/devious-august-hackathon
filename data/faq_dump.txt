KCC FREQUENTLY ASKED - UNSTRUCTURED DUMP
Assembled from call centre notes. Not reviewed. Contains duplicates.

Q: When should cotton be sown in Warangal Dist.?
A: Cotton sowing in Warangal Dist. is normally done from last week of
May up to about second week of July depending on rains.

Q: What is the seed rate for Bt cotton hybrid?
A: 1.5 kg per hectare.

Q: When should cotton be sown in Warangal?
A: The advised window is 10 June to 20 July for the northern zone.

Q: What is the pre harvest interval for fipronil on paddy?
A: Thirty days.

Q: Dose of fipronil 5 SC for gall midge in paddy?
A: 400 ml per acre. Note some references give this per hectare, check
the label before mixing.

Q: What is the seed rate for Bt cotton?
A: About 2 kg per hectare for hybrids under irrigated conditions.

Q: Paddy nursery area for one hectare?
A: 800 square metres.

Q: When to install pheromone traps for pink bollworm?
A: Forty five days after sowing, five traps per hectare.

Q: Soybean seed rate?
A: 65 to 75 kg per hectare.

Q: Soybean seed rate for medium seeded types?
A: Around 80 kg per hectare is commonly used by farmers.

Q: What is the spacing for Bt cotton under rainfed conditions?
A: 90 cm between rows and 60 cm between plants.

Q: When should paddy be transplanted?
A: 25 to 30 day old seedlings, mid June to end July.

Q: Nitrogen dose for paddy?
A: 120 kg per hectare in three splits.
