PACKAGE OF PRACTICES - COTTON (KHARIF)
Regional Agricultural Research Station extract

SOWING
Sowing is taken up with the onset of the south west monsoon. The
recommended sowing window for the northern Telangana zone is
10 June to 20 July. Sowing after the third week of July is not
advised as boll development coincides with declining moisture.

SPACING AND SEED RATE
Bt hybrids at 90 cm x 60 cm under rainfed conditions. Seed rate
1.5 kg per hectare for hybrids.

PLANT PROTECTION
Pink bollworm is monitored with pheromone traps at 5 traps per
hectare. Install traps 45 days after sowing. Spray only on crossing
the economic threshold level.
