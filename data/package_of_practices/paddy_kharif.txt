PACKAGE OF PRACTICES - PADDY (KHARIF)
Telangana State Agricultural Extension, Kharif 2025 advisory extract

NURSERY
Raise nursery on 800 sq m for one hectare of main field. Seed rate
for transplanted paddy is 30 kg per hectare for medium duration
varieties. Treat seed before sowing.

TRANSPLANTING
Transplant 25 to 30 day old seedlings. Spacing 20 cm x 15 cm.
Optimal transplanting window in northern Telangana districts is
mid June to end July. Delayed transplanting beyond the first week
of August reduces yield materially.

NUTRIENT MANAGEMENT
Apply 120 kg N, 60 kg P2O5 and 40 kg K2O per hectare. Nitrogen in
three splits: basal, active tillering, panicle initiation.

PLANT PROTECTION
Stem borer and leaf folder are the principal Kharif pests. Observe
the pre harvest interval on the label for every application. Do not
exceed the registered dose.
